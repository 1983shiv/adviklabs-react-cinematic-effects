new effect - coverflow

3D carousel with active card front and centre, flanking cards tilted
in perspective (ported from reference/coverflow.html). Click arrows
or cards to navigate; controlled + uncontrolled active index.

- src/effects/coverflow/: Coverflow.tsx, Coverflow.types.ts,
  useCoverflow.ts, Coverflow.module.css, index.ts
- register in src/index.ts, tsup.config.ts, package.json
  (./effects/coverflow)
- stories: src/stories/Coverflow.stories.tsx (Default, Dramatic
  Tilt, Flat Spread, Without Navigation)
- docs: Introduction.mdx + README.md (feature bullet, Quick Start,
  <Coverflow /> props table)
- build: npm run build passes (ESM + CJS + DTS)
